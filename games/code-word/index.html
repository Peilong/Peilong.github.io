Today’s word

Guess today’s computing term.

It has 4 to 7 letters, and you get one more guess than it has letters. Every guess must be a real word. After each guess, each letter gets a mark: ● right spot, ○ in the word but elsewhere, – not in the word.

The clock starts when you press Start.

What it means

Leaderboard

    Loading…

    Ranked by fewest guesses, then by time (measured by the server). Each name counts once a day; “Most days” counts the days a name made the board. Only the name you type and your result are saved, and names with bad words are refused or removed.

    How the clues work

    Every guess must be a real word with the same number of letters as the answer. After each guess, every letter gets a mark: a filled dot means the letter is in the right spot, a ring means the word has that letter somewhere else, and a dash means it doesn’t (or not that many times). The letter buttons keep track of what you have learned.

    About the words

    Each day’s word is a computing term that comes up in everyday life, from the cloud to cookies to chatbots. When the puzzle ends, you’ll see a short, plain explanation of what it means.
