Bit Flip

A one-minute game about how computers count. Flip the switches so they add up to the number.

How to play

Make the switches add up to the number.

Each switch is worth the number above it: to make 37, turn on 32, 4 and 1. You have 60 seconds. Answer within 5 seconds to build a streak and multiply your points.

On a keyboard: keys 1–8 flip the switches, Enter starts.

    What you just did

    Computers store every number as a row of on/off switches called bits. Each switch is worth double the one to its right: 1, 2, 4, 8, and so on up to 128. Eight of them together make a byte, which can hold any whole number from 0 to 255. The row of 0s and 1s under the number is that byte, written the way a computer stores it. So when you made 37, you wrote 00100101, which is 37 in binary.
